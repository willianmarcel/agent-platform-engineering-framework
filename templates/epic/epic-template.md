# Template — Epic

A blank form for an **epic**: a large body of work that groups related features toward a shared
outcome. Copy it to author an epic; populate every section (no placeholders). Completed epics live in
[`specifications/backlog/`](../../specifications/backlog/). Author the *what* and *why*, never the
*how*; name no technology.

## 1. Identity
- **Title** — the epic's name.
- **Owning authority** — the single role accountable for the epic.
- **Status** — Proposed | Accepted | In Progress | Done.

## 2. Outcome
The measurable business or platform outcome the epic exists to achieve.

## 3. Scope
- **In scope** — the work the epic covers.
- **Out of scope** — what it excludes.

## 4. Child features
The features that compose this epic, as links to their feature specifications.

## 5. Success criteria
Measurable criteria that determine the epic is complete.

## 6. Dependencies
Upstream capabilities or epics this depends on; downstream work that depends on it.

## 7. Traceability
Links to the originating capability or vision and to the
[Traceability model](../../specifications/SPECIFICATION_TRACEABILITY.md).

## Conventions
- One owning authority; measurable outcome and criteria; no technology named.

# Template — Story

A blank form for a **story**: a small, independently valuable increment within a feature, sized to be
completed and verified on its own. Copy it to author a story; populate every section (no
placeholders). Completed stories live in [`specifications/backlog/`](../../specifications/backlog/).
Author the *what* and *why*, never the *how*; name no technology.

## 1. Identity
- **Title** — the story's name.
- **Parent feature** — link to the owning feature.
- **Status** — Proposed | Ready | In Progress | Done.

## 2. Statement
The story from the actor's point of view: *as a &lt;actor&gt;, I need &lt;capability&gt; so that
&lt;outcome&gt;.*

## 3. Acceptance criteria
Measurable, verifiable criteria (given / when / then) that determine the story is done.

## 4. Scope & constraints
What the story includes and any constraints on it; what it explicitly excludes.

## 5. Child tasks
The tasks that implement this story, as links.

## 6. Traceability
Links to the parent feature and the
[Traceability model](../../specifications/SPECIFICATION_TRACEABILITY.md).

## Conventions
- Independently valuable and verifiable; measurable acceptance criteria; no technology named.

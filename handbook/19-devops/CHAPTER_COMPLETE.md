## Chapter Objective

Define DevOps — the engineering operations by which the platform is built, delivered, and operated — kept distinct from the platform runtime that executes business workloads.

## Scope

- **In scope:** DevOps, engineering operations, and platform operations; continuous integration, delivery, and deployment; deployment pipelines and release management; GitOps and the infrastructure lifecycle; and operational automation and readiness.
- **Out of scope (delegated to owning chapters):** the runtime execution of workloads (Chapter 07); the release philosophy (Chapter 20); and any pipeline, infrastructure, or automation tool.

## Concepts Owned

Chapter 19 is the authoritative source for: DevOps, Engineering Operations, Platform Operations, Continuous Integration, Continuous Delivery, Continuous Deployment, Deployment Pipelines, Release Management, GitOps, Infrastructure Lifecycle, Operational Automation, Operational Readiness. Future chapters may
reference these but must never redefine them.

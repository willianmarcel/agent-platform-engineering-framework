## Chapter Objective

Define the enduring engineering principles that govern how an AI Agent Platform is built — the standards a technical decision is held to, independent of technology, methodology, or architecture.

## Scope

- **In scope:** engineering, architectural, and design principles; engineering culture and technical excellence; and the core principles of simplicity, maintainability, evolvability, and quality by design.
- **Out of scope (delegated to owning chapters):** the methodology that applies the principles (Chapter 04), the architecture that results (Chapter 06), and any implementation or technology choice.

## Concepts Owned

Chapter 03 is the authoritative source for: Engineering Principles, Architectural Principles, Design Principles, Engineering Culture, Technical Excellence, Simplicity, Maintainability, Evolvability, Quality by Design. Future chapters may
reference these but must never redefine them.

## Chapter Objective

Define how the platform and the framework evolve over the long term — an evolution philosophy, not a project plan, a backlog, or a release schedule.

## Scope

- **In scope:** evolution strategy and long-term evolution; capability, product, and architectural evolution; backward compatibility, versioning strategy, release philosophy, technical-debt strategy, and innovation management.
- **Out of scope (delegated to owning chapters):** specific plans, backlogs, and schedules; the API versioning mechanism (Chapter 13); and release management operations (Chapter 19).

## Concepts Owned

Chapter 20 is the authoritative source for: Evolution Strategy, Long-term Evolution, Capability Evolution, Product Evolution Roadmap, Architectural Evolution, Backward Compatibility, Versioning Strategy, Release Philosophy, Technical Debt Strategy, Innovation Management. Future chapters may
reference these but must never redefine them.

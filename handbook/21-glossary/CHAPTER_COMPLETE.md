## Chapter Objective

Standardize the Handbook's terminology and provide the canonical vocabulary, owning terminology only and pointing every concept to its owning chapter.

## Scope

- **In scope:** canonical terminology, vocabulary, acronyms, and naming standards; and short definitions that gloss a term and point to its owning chapter.
- **Out of scope (delegated to owning chapters):** the definition of any engineering concept (owned by its chapter); the full per-concept index (maintained in the Handbook Index); and any implementation.

## Concepts Owned

Chapter 21 is the authoritative source for: Canonical Terminology, Definitions, Vocabulary, Acronyms, Naming Standards (terminology-management concepts only; the Glossary owns no engineering concepts). Future chapters may
reference these but must never redefine them.

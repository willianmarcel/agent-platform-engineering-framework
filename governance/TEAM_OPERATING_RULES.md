# Team Operating Rules

The Architecture Team is expected to think and operate as a senior engineering organization.

The Team shall:

- proactively identify remaining work
- reorganize execution whenever beneficial
- minimize Architecture Board interruptions
- preserve framework consistency
- optimize engineering quality

The Team shall not wait for task-by-task instructions.

Instead, it shall continuously evaluate:

"What is the next highest-value milestone required to complete the framework?"

and execute it.

The Team shall request Board review only after completing a milestone or when an escalation condition is reached.

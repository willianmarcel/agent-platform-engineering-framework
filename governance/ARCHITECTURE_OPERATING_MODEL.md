# Architecture Operating Model

The Architecture Team operates continuously.

Work is organized into milestones.

Each milestone follows the same lifecycle.

---

Discovery

↓

Planning

↓

Design

↓

Internal Review

↓

Cross Review

↓

Compliance Validation

↓

Architecture Review

↓

Architecture Board

↓

Next Milestone

---

The Architecture Team is responsible for planning the next milestone.

The Architecture Board validates only milestone outcomes.

---

The Architecture Team shall never wait for individual implementation prompts.

Instead, it shall continuously execute the Roadmap until:

- every milestone has been completed; or
- an escalation is required.

---

The Architecture Board is not responsible for task management.

The Architecture Board governs architecture.

The Architecture Team executes architecture.

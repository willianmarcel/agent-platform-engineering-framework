# Supervisor Examples

The worked supervisor examples of the APEF. They demonstrate a supervisor that coordinates and governs other agents toward a goal — a neutral artifact illustrating coordination, not implementation.

These examples demonstrate APEF concepts owned by [11](../../handbook/11-control-plane/CHAPTER.md), [07](../../handbook/07-runtime-platform/CHAPTER.md). They are **non-executable** and
**technology-neutral**: they name no vendor, product, framework, SDK, or implementation detail,
per the Architecture Board's permanent rule that examples demonstrate APEF concepts without
depending on any external technology. The directory [`README.md`](README.md) is a frozen
Foundation artifact and is unchanged; this document is the module entry point.

## Examples

- [Illustrative Coordinating Supervisor](./example-coordinating-supervisor.md) — demonstrates supervision/coordination, control-plane governance (11), runtime execution (07), human oversight (17).

## Conventions

- Non-executable artifacts and models only (specifications, structured definitions, diagrams,
  decision aids); never code, and never a specific technology.
- Concepts are referenced from their owning chapters, never redefined.

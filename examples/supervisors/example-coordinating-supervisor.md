# Example — Illustrative Coordinating Supervisor (conceptual)

A conceptual, **non-executable**, **technology-neutral** example demonstrating APEF concepts. It
defines no real product and names no vendor, product, framework, SDK, or implementation detail;
it illustrates methodology only.

**Intent.** Coordinate several agents toward a shared goal, delegating tasks and governing their work. *(illustrative)*

**Coordination.** Assigns tasks to agents and composes their results; a coordination concept informed by role-and-crew and hierarchical patterns (see the reference studies).

**Governance.** Applies control-plane policy to what the supervised agents may do ([Chapter 11](../../handbook/11-control-plane/CHAPTER.md)).

**Execution.** The supervisor and its agents are executed by the runtime ([Chapter 07](../../handbook/07-runtime-platform/CHAPTER.md)); the supervisor coordinates, it does not itself govern the platform.

**Human oversight.** A person can remain a deciding participant where judgment or risk require it.

**Acceptance criteria.** The goal is achieved through coordinated, governed agent work with oversight — measurable in a real specification.

## Concepts demonstrated

This example demonstrates supervision/coordination, control-plane governance (11), runtime execution (07), human oversight (17).

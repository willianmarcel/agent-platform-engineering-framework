# Engineering Guide

## Purpose
The Engineering Guide defines the engineering standards and practices that all APEF
contributions follow. It is the reference contributors consult to know *how* to do
engineering work in this framework to the expected level of quality.

## Contents
- Core engineering principles (maintainability, extensibility, long-term evolution).
- Documentation standards: structure, tone, linking, and the no-placeholder rule.
- How Specification-Driven Development is practiced day to day in this repository.
- Conventions for ADRs, specifications, architecture models, and diagrams.
- Review expectations and the definition of "done" for framework artifacts.

## Relationships
It elaborates the principles set in [`MASTER_PLAN.md`](MASTER_PLAN.md), is enforced by
[`QUALITY_GATES.md`](QUALITY_GATES.md), and is applied through the process in
[`WORKFLOW.md`](WORKFLOW.md). The handbook chapter
[`../handbook/03-engineering-principles/`](../handbook/03-engineering-principles/)
expands its underlying philosophy.

## Current state
Purpose-defined during Foundation. Full standards are authored in the Engineering phase.

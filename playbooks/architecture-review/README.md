# Playbook — Architecture Review

## Overview
A repeatable procedure for reviewing architecture proposals and models against the framework's principles and constraints.

## Purpose
To make this review repeatable and its outcomes consistent regardless of who runs it.

## Responsibilities
- Define the procedure's trigger, steps, decision criteria, and output.
- Enforce the relevant quality gates.

## Contents
- The review's trigger, prerequisites, checklist, decision criteria, and the record it produces.

# Playbook — Incident Review

## Overview
A repeatable procedure for reviewing incidents after the fact to learn and prevent recurrence.

## Purpose
To make this review repeatable and its outcomes consistent regardless of who runs it.

## Responsibilities
- Define the procedure's trigger, steps, decision criteria, and output.
- Enforce the relevant quality gates.

## Contents
- Postmortem structure, timeline reconstruction, root-cause analysis, and action tracking.

## Out of Scope
- Blameful narratives, secrets, or personal data.
- Live incident-response tooling.

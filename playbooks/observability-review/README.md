# Playbook — Observability Review

## Overview
A repeatable procedure for verifying that a design or change is adequately observable before it ships.

## Purpose
To make this review repeatable and its outcomes consistent regardless of who runs it.

## Responsibilities
- Define the procedure's trigger, steps, decision criteria, and output.
- Enforce the relevant quality gates.

## Contents
- An observability checklist covering metrics, logs, traces, alerts, and SLOs, plus review steps and records.

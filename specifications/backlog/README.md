# Specifications — Backlog

## Overview
Holds the structured backlog: epics, features, stories, and tasks derived from capabilities and the roadmap.

## Purpose
To track the units of work awaiting or in progress.

## Responsibilities
- Record the normative backlog specifications for the platform.
- Stay consistent with the vision and the ratified decisions.

## Contents
- Backlog items authored from the epic, feature, story, and task templates, with status and priority.

## Out of Scope
- [Blank templates.](../../templates/)
- [Accepted ADRs.](../../adrs/)

## Relationships
- [Templates](../../templates/) — the forms backlog items are authored from.
- [Capabilities](../capabilities/) — the source of backlog items.

## References
- [Master Plan](../../bootstrap/MASTER_PLAN.md) — the constitution governing the framework
- [Architecture Decisions](../../bootstrap/ARCHITECTURE_DECISIONS.md) — the ratified Foundation decision record

## Conventions
- Author specifications from the base specification template; back significant choices with an ADR.

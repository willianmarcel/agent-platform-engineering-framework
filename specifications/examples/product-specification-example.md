# Example — Product Specification (conceptual)

A conceptual, technology-neutral example illustrating **methodology only**. It shows how a
Product Specification is shaped; it defines no real product and names no technology.

- **Intent:** Enable an organization's experts to serve more requests consistently. *(illustrative)*
- **Personas & Jobs:** An operator who must handle a high volume of judgment-heavy requests.
- **Scope:** In — the judgment-heavy request handling. Out — unrelated administrative tasks.
- **Constraints:** Human oversight retained; outcomes accountable.
- **Acceptance Criteria:** Requests are handled to the defined standard, with oversight, at the
  target consistency. *(each criterion measurable in the real specification)*
- **Traceability:** Business objective → capability → this specification → testing/evaluation →
  acceptance.
- **Owning authority:** Product Architect; owning chapter
  [02](../../handbook/02-product-thinking/CHAPTER.md).

This example illustrates the *shape* of a compliant Product Specification, not a real product.

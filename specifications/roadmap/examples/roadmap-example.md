# Example — Roadmap Specification (conceptual)

A conceptual, technology-neutral example illustrating **methodology only** for the Roadmap
library. It shows the *shape* of a compliant Roadmap specification; it defines no real
product and names no technology.

- **Intent:** *(illustrative)* Specify how the product evolves: evolution strategy, capability evolution, release objectives, and strategic themes — a philosophy, not a schedule.
- **Content:** an illustrative Roadmap Specifications with its Evolution Strategy stated conceptually.
- **Acceptance Criteria:** each criterion measurable in the real specification.
- **Traceability:** derives from the backlog library;
  constrains the releases library.
- **Owner:** Product Architect / Enterprise Architect; owning chapter [Chapter 20](../../../handbook/20-roadmap/CHAPTER.md).

This example illustrates the methodology of a Roadmap specification, not a real product.

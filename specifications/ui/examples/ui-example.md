# Example — Experience Specification (conceptual)

A conceptual, technology-neutral example illustrating **methodology only** for the Experience
library. It shows the *shape* of a compliant Experience specification; it defines no real
product and names no technology.

- **Intent:** *(illustrative)* Specify the experience: interaction, navigation, accessibility, journeys, and human interaction, as philosophy rather than interface.
- **Content:** an illustrative Experience Specifications with its Interaction Specifications stated conceptually.
- **Acceptance Criteria:** each criterion measurable in the real specification.
- **Traceability:** derives from the observability library;
  constrains the backlog library.
- **Owner:** Product Architect / Technical Writer; owning chapter [Chapter 17](../../../handbook/17-ui-ux/CHAPTER.md).

This example illustrates the methodology of a Experience specification, not a real product.

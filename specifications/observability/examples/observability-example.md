# Example — Observability Specification (conceptual)

A conceptual, technology-neutral example illustrating **methodology only** for the Observability
library. It shows the *shape* of a compliant Observability specification; it defines no real
product and names no technology.

- **Intent:** *(illustrative)* Specify what must be observable and to what standard: metrics, logs, traces, health, monitoring, visibility, and observability acceptance.
- **Content:** an illustrative Observability Specifications with its Metrics stated conceptually.
- **Acceptance Criteria:** each criterion measurable in the real specification.
- **Traceability:** derives from the security library;
  constrains the ui library.
- **Owner:** Observability Architect; owning chapter [Chapter 14](../../../handbook/14-observability/CHAPTER.md).

This example illustrates the methodology of a Observability specification, not a real product.

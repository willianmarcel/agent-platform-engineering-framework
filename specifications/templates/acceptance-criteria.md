# Template — Acceptance Criteria

A reusable, normative section template of the APEF
[Specification Framework](../SPECIFICATION_FRAMEWORK.md): the section stating how satisfaction is confirmed. It is included within a full
specification rather than used alone.

**How to use:** Each criterion: a single, verifiable condition, marked as testable (software correctness) or evaluable (AI quality).

## Conventions
- Implementation-independent; names no technology.
- Consistent with [Traceability](../SPECIFICATION_TRACEABILITY.md),
  [Review](../SPECIFICATION_REVIEW.md), and [Completion](../SPECIFICATION_COMPLETION.md).

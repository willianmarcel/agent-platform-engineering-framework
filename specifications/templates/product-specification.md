# Template — Product Specification Template

A normative, implementation-independent template of the APEF
[Specification Framework](../SPECIFICATION_FRAMEWORK.md). It defines the required structure for
the product intent for a body of work. Copy it to author a specification; every section must be populated (no placeholders) to
pass the [completeness gate](../SPECIFICATION_COMPLETION.md).

**Purpose of this specification:** State the outcomes the work must deliver for identified personas and jobs.

## Required sections

1. **Intent** — the product outcome this work must deliver.
2. **Personas & Jobs** — who is served and what progress they seek.
3. **Scope** — in scope / out of scope.
4. **Constraints** — boundaries that must hold.
5. **Acceptance Criteria** — measurable outcome criteria.
6. **Traceability** — business objective; capabilities; reviews.
7. **ADR References** — significant decisions.
8. **Review & Completion** — dimensions applied; gates passed.

## Conventions
- State the *what* and *why*, never the *how*; name no technology, vendor, or language.
- Declare a single owning authority and the owning Handbook chapter (see the
  [Taxonomy](../SPECIFICATION_TAXONOMY.md)).
- Establish [traceability](../SPECIFICATION_TRACEABILITY.md) and pass the applicable
  [review dimensions](../SPECIFICATION_REVIEW.md) and [completion gates](../SPECIFICATION_COMPLETION.md).

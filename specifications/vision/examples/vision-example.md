# Example — Vision Specification (conceptual)

A conceptual, technology-neutral example illustrating **methodology only** for the Vision
library. It shows the *shape* of a compliant Vision specification; it defines no real
product and names no technology.

- **Intent:** *(illustrative)* Specify the enduring intent of a product: its vision, objectives, outcomes, and the constraints and assumptions that frame it.
- **Content:** an illustrative Vision Specifications with its Mission Specifications stated conceptually.
- **Acceptance Criteria:** each criterion measurable in the real specification.
- **Traceability:** derives from the product vision;
  constrains the discovery library.
- **Owner:** Product Architect / Enterprise Architect; owning chapter [Chapter 01](../../../handbook/01-platform-vision/CHAPTER.md).

This example illustrates the methodology of a Vision specification, not a real product.

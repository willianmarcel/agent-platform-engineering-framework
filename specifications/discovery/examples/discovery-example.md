# Example — Discovery Specification (conceptual)

A conceptual, technology-neutral example illustrating **methodology only** for the Discovery
library. It shows the *shape* of a compliant Discovery specification; it defines no real
product and names no technology.

- **Intent:** *(illustrative)* Specify what discovery has established before commitment: stakeholders, personas, problems, opportunities, assumptions, and open questions.
- **Content:** an illustrative Discovery Specifications with its Stakeholders stated conceptually.
- **Acceptance Criteria:** each criterion measurable in the real specification.
- **Traceability:** derives from the vision library;
  constrains the capabilities library.
- **Owner:** Product Architect; owning chapter [Chapter 02](../../../handbook/02-product-thinking/CHAPTER.md).

This example illustrates the methodology of a Discovery specification, not a real product.

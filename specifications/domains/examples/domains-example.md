# Example — Domain Specification (conceptual)

A conceptual, technology-neutral example illustrating **methodology only** for the Domain
library. It shows the *shape* of a compliant Domain specification; it defines no real
product and names no technology.

- **Intent:** *(illustrative)* Specify the problem domain: its bounded contexts, responsibilities, relationships, and boundaries, in ubiquitous language.
- **Content:** an illustrative Domain Specifications with its Bounded Context Specifications stated conceptually.
- **Acceptance Criteria:** each criterion measurable in the real specification.
- **Traceability:** derives from the capabilities library;
  constrains the architecture-requirements library.
- **Owner:** Domain Expert; owning chapter [Chapter 05](../../../handbook/05-domain-driven-design/CHAPTER.md).

This example illustrates the methodology of a Domain specification, not a real product.

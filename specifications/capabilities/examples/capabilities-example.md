# Example — Capability Specification (conceptual)

A conceptual, technology-neutral example illustrating **methodology only** for the Capability
library. It shows the *shape* of a compliant Capability specification; it defines no real
product and names no technology.

- **Intent:** *(illustrative)* Specify the durable capabilities a product provides, and their relationships, dependencies, boundaries, and evolution.
- **Content:** an illustrative Capability Specifications with its Capability Relationships stated conceptually.
- **Acceptance Criteria:** each criterion measurable in the real specification.
- **Traceability:** derives from the discovery library;
  constrains the domains library.
- **Owner:** Product Architect; owning chapter [Chapter 02](../../../handbook/02-product-thinking/CHAPTER.md).

This example illustrates the methodology of a Capability specification, not a real product.
